Hi, I am Irena Grocka

Lead Quality Engineer & Frontend Developer based in the UK

I'm a Lead Quality Engineer who helps teams build quality in from the start, rather than test it in at the end. Across enterprise programmes and SaaS product teams, I've championed shifting testing left, so risks surface early, feedback stays fast and releases go out with confidence. At Preservica I lead a team of quality engineers, shape our test automation strategy and drive the organisation's QE AI initiative as its AI Champion, improving not just what we test, but how we plan, build and ship.

Outside of work I keep building: I completed the SheCodes front-end development programme and enjoy crafting small, friendly web apps in JavaScript and React. You can find them in my projects portfolio. Every project is a chance to learn something new, and I approach each one with curiosity and a drive for quality.
